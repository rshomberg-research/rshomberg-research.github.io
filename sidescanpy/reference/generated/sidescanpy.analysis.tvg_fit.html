sidescanpy.analysis.tvg_fit#

TVG model fit: which spreading and absorption law the data still carries.

  • fit_tvg_models() — fit fixed TVG models (no TVG, absorption only, 20/30/40 log R, each with and without absorption) and a free spreading coefficient to the median level of raw data, over a window from past the profile peak to the noise knee (TVGFitResult).

  • check_tvg() — apply a TVG recipe to the data and measure how flat the corrected median level is over a window (TVGCheckResult).

The model is the sonar equation’s range dependence, L(R) = L0 - n log10 R - 2 alpha R, with the constant L0 (source level, gain and seabed strength together) fitted by least squares. The best model is the TVG to apply: its n and alpha are the spreading_loss_db and absorption_db_per_m of apply_tvg().

Bases:

TVG models fitted to the median level of raw data.

Profile: range_m (bin centers), level_db (both sides pooled, or the requested channel) and level_db_sides (port, starboard), the median of linear amplitude in dB re 1 recorded unit.

Window: window_m is (start, end); window_source is "knee" (peak plus start_margin_m to the noise knee), "end_of_record" (no knee found, fit to the end) or "given". peak_m, knee_m, knee_found, contrast_db, low_contrast and rises_at_far_range come from find_noise_knee().

Models: models maps each name to its TVGModelFit; best and runner_up are names, ranked by RMS in the window. free_n is the least-squares spreading coefficient with absorption (free_n_rms_db its RMS), free_n_no_absorption the same without. side_best holds each side’s fit (TVGSideFit) and sides_disagree flags sides whose levels or shapes differ.

Use: tvg_params is the best model as TVGParams (the TVG to apply); tvg_status names what the recorder has already removed ("none", "spreading_only", "absorption_only", "full"), derived from the best model. Both are None without a fit, and both are included in to_dict().

sidescanpy.analysis.tvg_fit.fit_tvg_models(ds, value_var='backscatter_intensity', range_var='slant_range_m', *, bin_width_m=1.0, start_margin_m=5.0, knee_db=3.0, window_m=None, min_window_m=8.0, low_contrast_db=6.0, spreading=(0.0, 20.0, 30.0, 40.0), absorption=(False, True), absorption_db_per_m=None, freq_hz=None, channel=None, dither='auto', seed=0, min_count=50, min_bins=3, side_difference_db=15.0, side_rms_tol_db=1.0, altitude_factor=2.5)#

Fit TVG models to the median level of raw backscatter.

  1. Profile: the median of linear amplitude in bin_width_m bins of range_var, past the median altitude (or, without altitude_m, outside the water column), both sides pooled (or channel), in dB re 1 (median_level_profile(); integer samples are dithered first).

  2. Window: from start_margin_m past the profile peak (searched in the near half of the range) to the noise knee, the first range within knee_db of the lowest level (find_noise_knee()). When no knee leaves min_window_m, the window runs to the end of the record, with a note; window_m overrides both ends.

  3. Models: every combination of spreading (n log R, 0 = none) and absorption (Francois-Garrison at the channel’s center frequency, or absorption_db_per_m), each with a least-squares constant, ranked by RMS residual in the window. A free n is fitted with and without absorption.

  4. Each side is fitted alone on the same window; sides_disagree is set (WARNING) when the two sides’ mean levels differ by more than side_difference_db, or the pooled best model’s shape fits a side side_rms_tol_db worse than that side’s own best.

A good fit is a hypothesis: confirm it with check_tvg() on the same window. Over short windows (under ~20 m) 20 log R with absorption, 30 log R and 40 log R differ by tenths of a dB; read free_n and the runner-up before trusting the label.

sidescanpy.analysis.tvg_fit.check_tvg(ds, tvg_params, window_m, value_var='backscatter_intensity', range_var='slant_range_m', *, crop_m=None, bin_width_m=1.0, channel=None, dither='auto', seed=0, min_count=50)#

Apply a TVG recipe and measure how flat the median level is.

The data are cropped at crop_m (default: the window’s far end; beyond it TVG only amplifies noise) with slice_by_range(), corrected with apply_tvg(), and binned like fit_tvg_models() (median of linear amplitude, dB). If the recipe is right the corrected level is flat across the window: spread_db near the fit’s RMS and trend_db_per_10m near zero. Validate on the same window the fit used.
